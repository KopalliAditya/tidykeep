# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

Tidykeep is built so that your photos and everything about them stay on your iPhone.

## What the app does with your photos

Tidykeep reads the photos in your library, on your device, to find near-identical shots
and suggest which ones to keep. It uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. Photos are
never uploaded, copied off the device or shared with anyone, including the developer.

Tidykeep does not download photos from iCloud. Photos that are only in iCloud are skipped.

## Faces

To prefer the frame where nobody blinked, Tidykeep asks Vision for a face capture quality
score: one number between 0 and 1 for each face in a photo. It does not recognize or identify
people, and it does not store face images or face geometry. The scores stay in the on-device
analysis described below, are never shared or used for anything else, and are erased by
Settings → Forget saved analysis or by deleting the app.

## What is stored

On your iPhone only, in the app's own storage:

- A compact analysis of each photo it has checked (a numeric fingerprint and quality
  scores), so photos aren't analyzed twice.
- Your review choices (which photos to keep, which groups you kept whole) and a count of
  photos removed.
- Your two settings (which photos to look at, and how alike they must be).

The analysis and your review choices are left out of device backups. The two settings are
ordinary app preferences and are included in backups. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
You can also clear the analysis at any time in Settings → Forget saved analysis.

## What is collected

Nothing. Tidykeep has no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no
tracking, and it makes no network requests.

## Removing photos

Tidykeep removes photos only when you ask, and iOS asks you to confirm each time. Removed
photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days.
If iCloud Photos is on, removed photos are also removed from iCloud and your other devices;
iOS says so in its confirmation.
